Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Hermes Personal is a private application used only by its owner to access the owner's own Google accounts.

Data accessed

With the owner's explicit consent, the app accesses Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Contacts data in the owner's own accounts.

How data is used

Data is used only to carry out tasks requested by the owner, such as searching mail or organizing labels. It is not used for advertising and is not sold, shared or transferred to any third party.

Storage

Authorization tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. The app operates no servers and keeps no copy of account data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
